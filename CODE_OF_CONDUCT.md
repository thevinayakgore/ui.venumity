# Community Code of Conduct

</div>

## Our Commitment

As contributors and maintainers of **Venu<span style="color:orange">mity</span> UI**, we are dedicated to encourage an open, inclusive, and respectful environment for everyone in our community - regardless of background, identity, or experience level. We pledge to create spaces where all participants feel safe, valued, and empowered to contribute.

## Community Standards

We expect all members of the **Venu<span style="color:orange">mity</span> UI** community to uphold the following principles :

### Expected Behavior

- **Show respect and empathy** in all interactions
- **Value diverse perspectives** and welcome constructive discussion
- **Provide thoughtful feedback** that helps others grow
- **Take ownership** of mistakes and learn from them
- **Prioritize collective success** over individual recognition

### Unacceptable Behavior

- **Harassment** in any form, whether public or private
- **Discriminatory language** or behavior based on identity
- **Personal attacks**, trolling, or deliberate provocation
- **Sharing private information** without explicit consent
- **Disrespecting boundaries** or making others feel uncomfortable
- **Any behavior** that undermines a safe, inclusive environment

## Community Leadership Responsibilities

Project maintainers are committed to :

- **Clearly communicating** and upholding these standards
- **Taking appropriate action** when standards are violated
- **Ensuring fairness** in all enforcement decisions
- **Respecting privacy** when handling reports
- **Continuously improving** our community culture

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to :

- All **Venu<span style="color:orange">mity</span> UI** project spaces (GitHub, Discord, forums, etc.)
- Public events where participants represent **Venu<span style="color:orange">mity</span> UI**
- Any communication officially linked to our community

## Reporting & Enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior :

**Report to :** thevinayakgore@gmail.com

All reports will be :
- **Reviewed promptly** and confidentially
- **Investigated fairly** with all parties involved
- **Addressed appropriately** based on the guidelines below

## Enforcement Guidelines

### 1. Correction
*For minor, unintentional violations*
- Private discussion clarifying community expectations
- Opportunity to learn and adjust behavior

### 2. Warning
*For repeated or more significant issues*
- Formal warning with clear expectations for change
- Temporary restrictions on community participation
- Monitoring of future interactions

### 3. Temporary Suspension
*For serious or ongoing violations*
- Temporary ban from community spaces
- No contact with affected individuals
- Clear path for potential reinstatement

### 4. Permanent Removal
*For severe violations or pattern of harmful behavior*
- Permanent removal from all community spaces
- Protection of community members from further harm

## Building Together

At **Venu<span style="color:orange">mity</span> UI**, we believe that great software is built by great communities. By participating here, you're helping create a space where developers can :
- **Learn** from each other
- **Build** incredible things together
- **Grow** both technically and personally
- **Innovate** without fear of judgment

Thank you for helping make **Venu<span style="color:orange">mity</span> UI** a welcoming place for everyone.
