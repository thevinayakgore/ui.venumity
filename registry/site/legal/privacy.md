#### Our Commitment to Privacy

At **Venumity UI** (`ui.venumity.com`), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information through a minimal data collection policy designed for software developers.

---

#### Information We Collect

**Information You Provide Directly :**

- **Account Information:** Email address, name, and profile details supplied during account registration.
- **Support Inquiries:** Information provided when contacting us through email, support forms, or GitHub Discussions.

**Automatically Collected Data :**

- **Analytics Data:** Anonymous, aggregated page-view metrics used to understand site performance and component usage.
- **Server Logs:** Basic IP addresses and technical logs necessary to diagnose server errors and prevent abuse or denial-of-service attacks.

---

#### Information We Do Not Collect or Store

- **Financial and Payment Card Information:** We do not collect, process, or store credit card numbers, debit card details, bank credentials, or UPI PINs.
- **Intrusive Tracking Data:** We do not track your browsing history across external websites or sell user data to third-party advertising brokers.

---

#### Third-Party Service Providers

We may integrate with trusted third-party services to operate and improve Venumity UI:

- **Clerk:** Manages user authentication and secure login sessions.
- **Vercel:** Hosts the frontend application and serverless backend API functions.

Each third-party service provider manages collected data according to its own privacy and security policies.

---

#### How We Use Your Data

We use collected information strictly to:

- Provide and maintain access to Venumity UI and its features.
- Authenticate users and secure accounts.
- Respond to support inquiries and resolve technical issues.
- Send essential transactional or security-related communications.

---
