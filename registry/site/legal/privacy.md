# Privacy Policy

## Our Privacy Commitment

At **Venu<span style="color:orange">mity</span> UI**, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

---

### 📊 Information We Collect

#### Information You Provide

- Email address when contacting us
- Feature requests and feedback
- Payment information (processed exclusively by Gumroad)

->

#### Automatically Collected

- Basic website analytics (anonymous)
- Error logs for debugging

->

### ❌ Information We Never Collect

- Payment details (handled securely by Gumroad)
- Sensitive personal information
- Browsing history across other sites
- Location data without consent
- Information from third-party trackers

---

### 🛠️ How We Use Your Information

#### We use collected information only to :

- Respond to your inquiries and support requests
- Process template purchases through Gumroad
- Improve our website and components
- Consider feature requests from the community
- Send purchased digital products

---

### 🔐 Data Protection

- **No Storage** : We don't store payment information
- **Limited Access** : Only necessary team members access data
- **Transparency** : We're open about our data practices

---

### 🗂️ Your Rights & Control

#### You have the right to :

- Request access to your personal information
- Ask for correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your information
- Opt-out of communications
- Understand how your data is used

---

### 🤝 Third-Party Services

- **Gumroad** : Handles all purchases and payment processing
- **GitHub** : Hosts our open-source components and discussions
- **Analytics** : Basic, anonymized website statistics
