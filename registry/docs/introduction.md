# Our Philosophy

At **Venu<span style="color:orange">mity</span> UI**, we believe in **developer‑first design**. Our components and resources are -

#### 🎯 Purpose‑Built
Every component solves a real problem we’ve encountered while building applications. No fluff, just functionality.

#### 🧩 Modular & Composable
Mix and match components like building blocks. Each component works independently and plays well with others.

#### 🎨 Customizable by Design
Use our components as‑is or modify them to fit your exact brand and design requirements.

#### 📱 Responsive from Day One
Every component is tested across mobile, tablet, and desktop breakpoints.

---

## Technology Stack

- **React 18+** – Modern React with hooks  
- **TypeScript** – Full type safety  
- **Tailwind CSS** – Utility‑first styling  
- **Framer Motion** – Smooth animations  
- **Lucide React** – Consistent iconography  
- **Next.js** (optional) – For documentation and examples

## Design Principles

- **Accessibility First** – All components follow WAI‑ARIA guidelines  
- **Performance Focused** – Minimal bundle size, optimised rendering  
- **Consistent API** – Predictable props and behaviour  
- **Clear Documentation** – Real‑world examples for every use case  
- **Learning by Doing** – Companion resource guides accelerate your learning
